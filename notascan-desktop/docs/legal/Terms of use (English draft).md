# Terms of use · NotaScan (school staff)

**Version 2026.1 · in force since October 6, 2026 · English translation of the Spanish version**

> **The Spanish version is the one that governs.** This English text is a technical draft translated by the development
> team. **A lawyer must review it before it is used.** If this text and the Spanish one differ, the Spanish one applies.

1. **Who can use it.** Only the staff the school registers (teachers, academic office, principal's office). The account
   is personal and cannot be transferred. Do not share your password.
2. **Allowed use.** Use NotaScan only for your work at the school. Student and guardian data belongs to the school
   community. Do not copy it, publish it or use it for other purposes.
3. **The AI only suggests.** The automatic reading of photos is a help. **You verify each grade.** The grade that counts
   is the one you confirm.
4. **Your actions are signed.** NotaScan records who verifies grades, takes attendance, writes notes or decides
   requests. That record is part of the academic history.
5. **Sensitive data.** Only the academic office and the principal's office see health and guardian data. If you access
   it, use it only for what it was authorized for.
6. **Working offline.** Grades written offline stay saved on your computer until they are sent. Do not use shared
   computers without signing out.
7. **Availability.** NotaScan tries to work without interruptions, but there may be maintenance or failures in the
   services it depends on.
8. **Changes.** If these terms or the data policy change, you will be asked to accept them again when you sign in.
9. **Personal data.** The current *Personal data processing policy* applies.
